## High-Clarity Typography Correction

The corrected interface was reviewed across the hero, lesson workspace, private reflection block, Reading Detective, and Phrase Mosaic. The decorative oversized Japanese watermark, heavy card rotation, and high-density card presentation were replaced with restrained navigation, standard reading sizes, high-contrast navy text, clear Japanese glyph rendering, and bounded white learning surfaces. Tunisian Arabic guidance remains legible against plain surfaces with clear separation from Japanese and supporting Latin copy.

The desktop composition keeps the dialogue and reflection workspace distinct. The responsive rules collapse the level, activity, and phrase grids at narrow widths to prevent compressed text and preserve native keyboard controls.
